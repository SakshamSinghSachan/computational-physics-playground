01Stream function and Helmholtz decomposition

A field built as F = curl(ψ ẑ) is divergence free identically; a field with nonzero divergence admits no stream function; and any periodic field splits uniquely into an irrotational and a divergence free piece.

Method notes

Two bugs produced confident, wrong output and are documented in the notebook because both are easy to repeat.

A wrong sign in the irrotational piece. The potentials φ and ψ were correct, but the reconstruction used −∇φ instead of +∇φ. Every per-component check still passed; only the reconstruction residual exposed it, at 1.2 — larger than the field itself.

An analytic reference that was wrong. Differentiating sin x e−0.3y² as though the first factor were x gives the wrong Laplacian. The error was constant under refinement, which is the signature of a bad reference rather than a bad scheme — and a convergence table that refuses to move is what catches it.
